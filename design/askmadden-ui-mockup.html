How the new flow works

Login — asks for a Sleeper username only, not a league ID. That's the real Sleeper-side lookup: username → user_id → every league that user_id belongs to for the season.

Picker — shows every league Sleeper returns for that username as a card (name, format, your team in it). Tapping one lands you straight on Feed for that league.

Toggle — same "Your leagues" list reopens from the league avatar in the header. Switching updates the header and, in the real build, re-runs the per-league join in recommend.py — signals themselves don't need to be recomputed.

Fallback — "Paste a league ID instead" stays available on the picker for leagues you don't personally belong to.
